ADMISSION GUIDELINES

Your Journey Towards Medical Education Starts Here

Explore the standard 5-step admission roadmap, statutory eligibility criteria, entrance examination prerequisites, and counselling procedures for medical aspirants in India.

5-STEP TIMELINE

Standard Medical Admission Process

Procedural progression from entrance qualification to institutional enrollment under Indian regulatory frameworks.

01

Step 01: Review Eligibility

Aspirants evaluate essential academic qualifications: successful completion of 10+2 higher secondary examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English from a recognized educational board, adhering to age limits and aggregate benchmarks.

10+2 Higher Secondary Science Stream (PCB) Prescribed Age Compliance
02

Step 02: Entrance Examination

Candidates appear for the mandatory national competitive entrance exam (NEET-UG for undergraduate MBBS; NEET-PG for postgraduate MD/MS) administered by authorized national examination bodies.

National NEET Examination Merit Cut-off Percentile Official Scorecard
03

Step 03: Counselling and Seat Allocation

Eligible candidates register on authorized central or state counselling portals. Seat allocation proceeds through multi-round merit-based choice locking governed by statutory counselling committees.

Central/State Counselling Merit Choice Locking Seat Allocation Order
04

Step 04: Document Verification

Allotted candidates report with original academic certificates, entrance admit card, rank card, transfer certificates, category documentation (if applicable), and medical fitness certificate.

Original Transcripts NEET Rank Letter Physical Fitness Clearance
05

Step 05: Admission Confirmation

Remittance of statutory regulatory tuition fees, receipt of formal institutional enrollment number, participation in the institutional white coat ceremony, and commencement of academic orientation.

Institutional Enrollment White Coat Orientation Academic Timetable
Statutory Regulatory Notice

Medical admission procedures, eligibility, entrance examination requirements, counselling, seat allocation, and applicable regulations depend strictly on current official rules and relevant authorities. This website does not guarantee admission, offer direct seats, or invent institutional criteria.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Eligibility Information for Indian Medical Aspirants

Key educational prerequisites for undergraduate and postgraduate medical applicants in accordance with national norms.

Undergraduate (MBBS) Eligibility Overview

General statutory norms applicable to candidates seeking admission into MBBS programs

Requirement Prescribed Statutory Guidelines Verification Advisory
Qualifying Examination Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent from a recognized Central/State educational board. Original pass certificates and marksheets required during physical verification.
Mandatory Subjects Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English as core compulsory subjects. Candidate must have passed each subject individually.
Minimum Qualifying Marks Minimum qualifying aggregate marks in PCB as prescribed by current statutory authorities (e.g. general category benchmarks). Criteria varies for reserved categories as per current official regulatory guidelines.
Entrance Examination Qualifying score in the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG). Scorecard and all-India overall merit rank letter issued by the conducting agency.
Age Criteria Must have attained the minimum prescribed age (generally 17 years on or before December 31 of the admission year). Birth certificate or secondary school certificate (10th) serving as age proof.

Postgraduate (MD / MS) Eligibility Overview

General criteria applicable to medical graduates seeking postgraduate residency specializations

Requirement Prescribed Statutory Guidelines Verification Advisory
Basic Medical Degree Recognized MBBS degree from an institution approved under prevailing medical education statutes. Degree certificate / provisional pass certificate.
Internship Completion Satisfactory completion of 1-year Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship (CRMI) on or before official cut-off date. Internship completion certificate issued by the teaching institution.
Statutory Registration Permanent or provisional registration certificate issued by the National Medical Commission or State Medical Council. Valid medical council registration number mandatory.
Postgraduate Entrance Qualifying in the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for Postgraduates (NEET-PG). NEET-PG scorecard and merit seat allocation letter.
Regulatory Verification Disclaimer

Exact eligibility criteria, category relaxations, syllabus details, and counselling dates must be verified against current official regulations issued by statutory national bodies and designated examination authorities. Do not rely on third-party portals for definitive legal requirements.
