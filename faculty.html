PEDAGOGICAL EXCELLENCE

Nurturing Medical Learners Through Compassionate Guidance

Medical education requires close mentorship where seasoned academicians guide aspiring doctors through complex pathophysiological concepts, clinical reasoning, and ethical patient engagement.

Under the Competency-Based Medical Education (CBME) paradigm, faculty mentors utilize interactive small-group discussions, early clinical exposure (ECE), simulation workshops, and continuous formative feedback.

Small-Group Tutoring

Batch mentoring facilitating individual clinical skill refinement.

Ethical Role Modeling

Instilling values of patient dignity, bioethics, and lifelong inquiry.
